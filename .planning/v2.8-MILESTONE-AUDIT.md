## The one thing actually blocking closure

`release-please` is red on **every** run on `main` — push and schedule alike, confirmed live on
2026-09-18 across the five most recent runs. The cause is **not** a v2.8 regression and **not** the
old secondary rate limit (`gh api rate_limit` reads 5000/5000):

Release proposal **PR #280** (opened 2026-09-17) proposes `2.6.0/2.6.0/2.3.0` — versions already
tagged and published. Preflight short-circuits on *"All expected release tags already exist"*,
skipping the release-please action **and** the discovery/capture steps, so the proposal-only control
falls through to its default `cannot-check` / `github_evidence_unavailable` and fails closed.

Failing closed is correct behavior. But it means CTRL-02 and CTRL-03 cannot be *observed* until #280
is closed or retargeted and a fresh proposal merges — a release-ceremony decision that belongs to a
human, and one this milestone deliberately declined to fake.

**Disposing of #280 unblocks CTRL-02 and CTRL-03 together.** The other two partials close on their
own: GREEN-05 Part 2 on the next `main` CI run with `mix.lock` unchanged, CTRL-05 on the
2026-09-19 and 2026-09-20 cron firings.
