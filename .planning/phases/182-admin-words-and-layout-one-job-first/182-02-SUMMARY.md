## Accomplishments

- An operator can type the customer's address into a labelled `Email address` search that now sits above the collapsible filter panel at every width, land on one row that reads Failed (mark and word), open the Quick view line verdict, and open Full detail whose first block is the verdict (Next step, cause when recorded), all proven in the required lane.
- The verdict is pure data in `Copy.delivery_verdict/2` fed by `StatusMap.delivery_class/1`; rows, Quick view and Full detail cannot disagree. Every Sent-class verdict says sent is not delivered, a queued row says `Not handed to the provider yet.`, and a row from an older core says `Status cannot be fully determined with this mailglass version.` with an Unknown badge.
- Cause text cannot be produced for a nil reason (no clause), the fixed `No reason was recorded for this email.` covers provider bounces and rejections with a nil reason, a send-time failure keeps its true handoff sentence, and an unloaded or unreadable history shows neither.
- The browser localizer now formats with `en-US` and a zone token, and no longer writes an `aria-label` that drops the visible text; the copy control's name contains the local text.
- Tooltips no longer promise a reason or an error that may not exist, the suppression card's nil Reason reads the fixed fallback, and the gallery drops its retry-window wording.

An unlisted reason from a newer core renders "The recorded reason is not one this version recognises." rather than crashing the screen.
