<load_bearing_invariant_callout>
## ⚠ D-05 — THE SINGLE HIGHEST-RISK ITEM IN THE PHASE

When `theme_picker` adopts the `set_theme` event, its redirect MUST route through Preview's OWN
**`preview_theme_path/2`** (`preview_live.ex:590-605`, which calls `put_frame_query` →
restored by `frame_from_params` on remount), and MUST NOT call the operator shell's
**`set_theme_path/2`** (`shell.ex:102-117`), which has NO frame handling and would silently reset the
email backdrop on every chrome-theme flip.

The `admin_chrome_theme` persists via a full controller redirect through `/theme/<t>` that remounts
the LiveView and drops the in-memory `preview_frame_dark_chrome` — `frame=dark` in `return_to` is what
keeps the backdrop alive across the remount. **The `return_to` you build in the new `set_theme` handler
MUST keep the `put_frame_query(socket.assigns.preview_frame_dark_chrome)` call.**

`flows.spec.js:454-458` (frame→dark flips `preview-pane` `data-preview-frame-theme="dark"` while
`preview-shell` stays `data-theme="mailglass-light"`) locks this end-to-end. It MUST stay green and
MUST NOT be weakened.
</load_bearing_invariant_callout>
