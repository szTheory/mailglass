## Decisions Made

- `delivery_class/1` ships in this phase over a map with a `:status` key, honouring durable fields when present (amended precedence: failure report or durable failure, suppression, confirmed delivery, send-time failure, deferral, sent).
- `Copy` keeps one sanctioned fallback for the status badge only; every strict reader raises for `nil` and `:subscribed`.
- `vocabulary/0` marks only `:account` as `wired: true` (the word "Account" is already on screen); the Operator, Activity and orphan words and all five class labels are `wired: false` data.
- Layer 2 compares rendered text with `Copy`, and layer 1 owns the literal values, so a `Copy` label change is caught by layer 1 and a hardcoded literal by layers 2 and 3.
