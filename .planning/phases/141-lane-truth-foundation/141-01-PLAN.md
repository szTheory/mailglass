## Decisions recorded here (never made by omission)

Per `.planning/research/v2.2/PITFALLS.md:488-494`, a behavior change must never ride inside a
cosmetic edit. These three choices are stated explicitly rather than left implicit:

1. **The drift meta-test is wired into `mix_task_tests`, NOT `support_contract_core`.**
   `mix_task_tests` is publish-gating (absent from `ci_green.needs`); `support_contract_core` is
   merge-gating (present in `ci_green.needs`). Wiring to `support_contract_core` would make registry
   drift block a PR merge — a change to what blocks a merge, which CONTEXT.md's `<domain>` block puts
   explicitly out of scope for Phase 141. Consequence accepted: drift blocks a **release**, not a PR.
   Free bonus: this also promotes the two existing meta-tests (GATE-03, MIXCI-03) from cron-only
   advisory to publish-gating.

2. **The alias carries `--warnings-as-errors`, matching every sibling `verify.*` alias.**
   Dropping the flag would silently exempt `test/scripts/` from a convention every other lane honors —
   a small instance of the exact problem this phase exists to fix (RESEARCH.md **F5**). The pre-existing
   dead assertion that blocks the flag is therefore removed as part of this task, not worked around.

3. **`required_checks_test.exs` is NOT refactored to delegate to the new `Mailglass.CIYaml` module.**
   Its five parsers stay `defp` and stay duplicated (~20 lines). GATE-03 is the gate this phase's
   correctness depends on; refactoring it mid-phase risks exactly the collateral damage the milestone
   exists to prevent (RESEARCH.md §Parser reuse). Accepted debt, recorded in the new module's doc.
