<objective>
Swap the admin to the brand v2 fonts through the sha256 brand pin and serve them from a compile-time embedded allow-list (BRTOK-03).

Purpose: The admin ships Inter / Inter Tight / IBM Plex at 400/700 and reads fonts from disk per request. Brand v2 uses Funnel Display 600, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400/600/400i and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono 400/600 (six woff2, 52,508 bytes, D-02). This plan extends the pin with one idempotent `extend` verb (D-18), lands the six woff2 + three OFL texts + three brand SVGs, deletes the retired fonts, embeds the new fonts at compile time (D-11), maps `font-bold` to 600 (D-09), sets faces by role with tabular numerals on email list rows (D-10), proves it statically in the required lane (D-12), and fixes every coupled file list and evidence script that a required or gsd lane reads (D-14, F2, F3, F5). The advisory e2e and demo font names, and the advisory D-12 browser run, move to 181-04 Task 2 (see `<decisions_recorded>`).

Output: `extend` verb and tests, updated pin, new font/OFL/SVG files, compile-time font map, rebuilt bundle, marker, `font_assets_contract_test.exs`, updated bundle/assets tests, publish allowlist, evidence scripts, tabular-nums on the two list containers.
</objective>
