RESEARCH.md § "The Premise Has Moved" (`:186-247`) measured all four legs on `main` at SHA `3edc95f0`
(run `30464215272`): 1.18/public 29 failures / **0** `already_shared`; 1.18/mailglass 12 / **0**;
1.19/public 213 / **354**; 1.19/mailglass 22 / **0**. The `:already_shared` signature is **absent from both
