<objective>
Route the nine `:auto`-mode files through `SandboxOwnership.unsandboxed_module/1` so their mode switch and
revert are paired by construction, without changing what any of them does.

These nine are not the leak — they are the reason the lane was 194-red rather than 668-red. Each one
*heals* a leaked shared owner on its way in, which is why leaker and victim are never adjacent and why seed
bisection converges on the wrong pair. The migration makes the pairing deterministic and brings them under
one enforceable door; it must not remove the healing.

Purpose: without this, the Credo check in plan `143-08` cannot include `:mode` in its forbidden-function
list, and nine files keep a hand-rolled acquire/revert pair that a tenth file will copy.
Output: nine files on `setup :unsandboxed_module`, with the two non-trivial-teardown files verified
individually.
</objective>
