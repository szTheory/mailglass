mailglass — logo tournament, round 3 (final)

Four candidates on the locked 8F direction: the imagery inside the mark, plus the color program. Round 2 asked whether the identity is mono-only — mono was a constraint test; this round introduces the token-only color program (Ink, Glass, Ice, Mist — no gradients, no new hexes, no opacity). Every candidate keeps a single-path even-odd mono master as the canonical fallback; the color versions are layered paths over the same geometry. The wordmark is the round-1 paths verbatim throughout.

Design examination

8F was re-examined large, small, and in color before anything was drawn. The two-tone split does real work: giving the lens its own hue separates pane from lens at a glance, so the lit corner reads as shared light even faster than in mono — and on dark, the Mist-pane/Ice-lens expression is the strongest render of this mark to date. The geometry needed no correction, so 8F-1 ships it untouched as the control. Filling the void Ice (8F-2) produces a convincing transparent-lens read with zero opacity tricks, at the cost of the void’s airiness; it is the only candidate that changes how the light itself behaves — and on dark its Glass lens deliberately recedes so the light outshines the glass, which softens the circle gestalt at small dark sizes. The message line (8F-3) pulls the pane toward document vocabulary when judged alone, but the corner lens anchors it in the 8F language, and the line simplifies away by design at favicon size. The through-light (8F-4) doubles the lights to picture transit — in one corner, out the other — buying the most distinctive silhouette in the field at the cost of the baseline’s calm.
