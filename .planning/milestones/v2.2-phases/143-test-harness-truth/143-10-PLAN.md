<objective>
Pin the anti-vacuity thresholds from real green CI evidence and turn enforcement on in the lane.

This is the plan where the numbers stop being placeholders. D-27 is explicit: floors may only be pinned from
green CI runs, never locally — and the environment makes that binding rather than stylistic, because the
local toolchain is Elixir 1.19.5 while the gating legs run 1.18.4, so a local measurement would be taken on
the wrong leg of the very matrix the floor guards.

Purpose: a floor measured from a red run is a floor measured against a broken suite. A floor with a safety
margin is slop that absorbs the first regression silently.
Output: measured per-schema floors, a measured skipped ceiling, the env opt-in on both lane steps, and a
drift test that fails if either half of the guard disappears.
</objective>
