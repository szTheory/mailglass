<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Resolver facts -> SPF/DKIM/DMARC analyzers | External TXT/CNAME answers become operator-facing findings |
| Explicit DKIM selectors -> selector checks | User-provided selector strings become DNS names under `_domainkey` |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-25-05 | Integrity | SPF analyzer | mitigate | Count lookup pressure and void lookups explicitly so nested includes cannot be misreported as healthy `pass` results. |
| T-25-06 | Tampering | DKIM analyzer | mitigate | Require explicit selectors and treat missing selectors as `cannot_verify`; do not infer provider folklore or silently rewrite selector inputs. |
| T-25-07 | Denial of Service | malformed TXT/tag parsing | mitigate | Parse TXT/tag data defensively and turn malformed SPF, DKIM, or DMARC records into `fail` or `cannot_verify` findings rather than crashes. |
| T-25-08 | Repudiation | remediation semantics | mitigate | Keep finding evidence and observed strings tied to the exact protocol area and selector/policy under test so later JSON/human output can explain why a status was assigned. |
</threat_model>
