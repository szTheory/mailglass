mailglass v0.1 is the validation release: `mailglass` core + `mailglass_admin` dev preview shipped to Hex.pm. Seven planned phases tracing the 7-layer build order from `research/SUMMARY.md`, plus one inserted phase (07.1) for the publish-to-Hex.pm cycle. All 84 v1 REQ-IDs satisfied.
