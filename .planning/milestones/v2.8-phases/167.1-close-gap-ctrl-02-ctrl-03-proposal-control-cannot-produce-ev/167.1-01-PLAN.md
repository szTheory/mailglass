repository?", so the proposal-only release control can reach a truthful verdict in the permanent
post-close-out steady state instead of failing `cannot-check` / `github_evidence_unavailable` on every
push and every hourly schedule.

those clauses require; Plan 02 records the harvest procedure for that.

all five reachable world states plus the two guards that must not move.
