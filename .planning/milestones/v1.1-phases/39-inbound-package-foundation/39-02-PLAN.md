<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| normalized record -> raw evidence | Stable adopter-facing truth must not be contaminated by raw provider payload and verification data. |
| stored evidence -> replay runner | Later reprocessing will trust persisted evidence to represent a prior receive honestly. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-39-04 | Information Disclosure | canonical/evidence schemas | mitigate | Keep raw MIME, payload JSON, selected raw ingress headers, verification facts, and parse warnings inside `InboundEvidence`, not on the canonical row or public struct, per D-39-20 and D-39-21. |
| T-39-05 | Elevation of Privilege / Tampering | tenant boundary and FKs | mitigate | Require `tenant_id` on every persisted row and restrict relationships to package-local FKs only, with no links into `mailglass` tables, per D-39-23. |
| T-39-06 | Repudiation | replay lineage | mitigate | Store replay as a separate append-only `ReplayRun` linked to existing evidence so later execution cannot masquerade as a fresh receive per D-39-22 and D-39-24. |
</threat_model>
