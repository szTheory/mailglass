<objective>
Restructure `support_cards.ex` from a flat equal-weight 2×2 grid into the two-tier hierarchy specified in 74-UI-SPEC.md — Tier 1 full cards for actionable/non-zero counts, Tier 2 compact row for zero-state/informational items — and apply token-clean markup to the final structure (restructure-first, then tokenize, per D-07/Pitfall 4).

Purpose: The flat grid (GAP-13 sev 4) gives equal visual weight to every card regardless of triage signal, making it impossible to see at a glance whether any action is required. Tier 1 cards with semantic color counts communicate urgency; Tier 2's compact row communicates "nothing to act on" without visual clutter.

Output: `support_cards.ex` with Tier1/Tier2 markup + token-clean spacing and typography; the flat grid is eliminated.

Anti-churn citations: GAP-13 (sev 4), GAP-14 (sev 3).
</objective>
