<objective>
Resolve the new Plan 22 stop cause locally by installing Linux's existing `inotify-tools` package only in the `preview_capture_advisory` job, protect that workflow contract with a focused test, and freeze a new local candidate with authority unset.

Purpose: Phoenix's `file_system` watcher uses Linux inotify and reports a setup failure when its watcher executable is missing. Installing the OS package in this one advisory job preserves the project's Elixir/npm dependency graph and application configuration.

Output: A reviewed source commit, saved local static-check evidence, and a separate `178-RECOVERY-CYCLE-02.json` record binding a new candidate SHA to its source bytes while granting no hosted or preview authority.

This plan does not clear or rewrite Plan 22's stop, does not run Plans 23/24, does not calculate hosted cost, and does not perform any remote action or preview launch. The old candidate `f5138e94cd32daf29720f794cf3fbb464c3f1b86` and its consumed three-operation approval remain historical and unchanged. Any later hosted operation requires a new approval on the exact candidate and operation list.
</objective>
