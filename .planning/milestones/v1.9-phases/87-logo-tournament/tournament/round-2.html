Design examination

At display sizes the baseline's gestalt is exactly right — square and circle read instantly as pane and lens, and the voided overlap reads as light, not damage, because the solid outer half completes the circle. The mass balance is off, though: a 96-unit solid square against 22-unit type stems makes the mark a heavy block beside an airy word. The equal-sided sharp square drifts toward folder and sticky-note vocabulary at 32 px and below, where the lens shrinks to a bump on the edge. At 16 px the motif nearly disappears — the void subtends about three pixels and the 14-unit overshoot below the pane reads as noise rather than a boundary break. The 52-unit gap sits exactly at the 0.4 × mark-width bound and leaves mark and word reading as neighbors rather than one unit. Each variant below attacks one of these findings; the spinoff resolves them together.
