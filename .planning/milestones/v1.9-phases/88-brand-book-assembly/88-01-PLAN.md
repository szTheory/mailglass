WCAG math: relative luminance per WCAG 2.x (sRGB channels /255, linearize
c <= 0.04045 ? c/12.92 : ((c+0.055)/1.055)^2.4, L = 0.2126R+0.7152G+0.0722B),
ratio = (Lmax+0.05)/(Lmin+0.05), displayed rounded to 2 decimals.
Verdicts: AA normal >= 4.5, AAA normal >= 7.0, AA large >= 3.0, AAA large >= 4.5.
