# Mailglass Logo Decision Record

Status: selected identity direction.

The active Mailglass logo direction is:

`assets/concepts/concept-07r-no-idot-02-tighter-gap.svg`

This replaces the earlier exploration posture. Future work should refine
exports, usage documentation, and small presentation details without reopening
the rejected i-dot or option-round territory.

## Brand Center

Mailglass makes email visible.

Mail you can see through.

Glass is a metaphor for clarity, inspection, trust, and visibility. It is not a
reason to add shine, refraction tricks, decorative transparency, or
glassmorphism.

## Selected Logo Principle

Use the mark plus a full untouched wordmark.

The selected lockup keeps the existing mail-under-glass mark and pulls it closer
to the wordmark. That tighter gap makes the mark and name read as one logo,
while the wordmark remains a single `mailglass` text node.

The name does the identity work. The mark supports it.

## Hard Constraints

Do not use:

- i-dot variants
- masks, clip paths, boolean cuts, or dot cuts
- dotless glyphs
- split words or tspans
- fake i stems
- panes over the wordmark
- paper/document/card-only logo reads
- envelope-only, paper plane, mailbox, chat bubble, send arrow, glossy app icon,
  mascot, decorative gradient, or glassmorphism directions
- visual borrowing from Elixir, Phoenix, Rust, Git, Tailwind, Vite, Kubernetes,
  React, or Svelte

## Archived Lessons

- A/B/C looked like paper, documents, or cards.
- D/E/F were too narrow and too close to wordmark-header treatments.
- G-R became low-quality UI snippets, diagrams, or generic devtool marks.
- The later literal mail + glass rounds found the right structure: mark plus
  wordmark.
- The i-dot idea failed because fake stems looked wrong, SVG cuts harmed nearby
  letters, and dotless salvage felt fussy.

## Asset Direction

Canonical assets should stay source-native SVG under `brandbook/`.

The approved asset family is:

- primary lockup
- mark
- monochrome/currentColor mark
- favicon
- social avatar

Each asset should keep unique title/description IDs, no raster images, no
embedded fonts, and no unsafe SVG constructs.
