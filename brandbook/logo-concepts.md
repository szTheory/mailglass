# Mailglass Logo Selection Notes

Status: selected direction recorded. The active logo direction is
`assets/concepts/concept-07r-no-idot-02-tighter-gap.svg`.

Open `logo-concepts.html` for the visual board. This Markdown file records the
decision and the constraints that must survive future asset work.

## Selected Direction

Selected file:

`assets/concepts/concept-07r-no-idot-02-tighter-gap.svg`

Decision:

- Use the existing mail-under-glass mark.
- Use the tighter mark-to-wordmark gap from no-i-dot 02.
- Keep the wordmark untouched as one full `mailglass` text node.
- Do not manipulate the i dot.

Why this wins:

- The closer mark-to-wordmark spacing makes the lockup feel like one logo, not
  two adjacent parts.
- The name remains the identity anchor.
- The mark adds the visible-mail cue without placing decorative panes over the
  wordmark.
- The construction stays source-native SVG and works across light, dark,
  monochrome, mark-only, and compact checks.

## Constraints

Do not reintroduce:

- i-dot variants
- masks or boolean cuts
- dotless glyphs
- split words or tspans
- fake i stems
- panes over the wordmark
- paper/document/card-only marks
- envelope-only, paper plane, mailbox, chat bubble, send arrow, glossy app icon,
  mascot, decorative gradient, or glassmorphism directions
